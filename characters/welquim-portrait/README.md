# Halftone portrait avatar

Generated with the built-in image generation tool from the supplied portrait,
`reference.png`. The requested photographic proportions and monochrome halftone
style take precedence over the page-mascot skill's default cartoon style.

`directions.png` and `reactions.png` are the source sheets. The page-mascot build
aligns their shoulders and exports the two WebP atlases to `public/mascots/`.
The center direction cell also supplies `welquim-portrait-avatar.webp` for the
static profile photos. The previous cartoon assets remain available.

Rebuild from the frontend directory with Python containing Pillow, NumPy and SciPy:

```powershell
& ./.data/mascot-tools/python/python.exe C:/Users/welpa/.agents/skills/page-mascot/scripts/mascot.py welquim-portrait --skip-generate
```

## Direction prompt

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: production cursor-following portrait sprite atlas for a personal portfolio. Input image 1 is the identity AND style reference, preserve this exact man's likeness and distinctive facial features. Generate ONE square PNG containing an evenly spaced 3 columns by 3 rows grid, exactly NINE portraits. A second matching expression atlas will follow. Preserve the reference's realistic adult proportions, recognizable face, short tousled wavy dark hair, faint thin moustache, warm slight smile, dark suit jacket, white shirt and diagonally striped tie, and BLACK AND WHITE finely dotted photographic halftone/stipple print treatment. This is a realistic halftone portrait, never a cartoon or chibi, no glasses. Background genuinely transparent alpha, including all gutters. Each cell is the same close portrait bust: head, neck, upper shoulders only, no arms or hands. Head around 60 percent of bust height, shoulders at most 1.45 times head width. The torso is front-facing, EXACTLY the same drawing, width, size, location, shoulder line, shirt and tie pixels in all nine cells. Only the head actually rotates in 3D naturally at the neck. The expression stays the same. Substantial clear directional change, yaw about 30 degrees left/right, pitch about 22 degrees up/down, enough to see the cheek/nose perspective changing. All directions are FROM THE VIEWER'S PERSPECTIVE: row 1 = turn face toward upper LEFT of image, look straight UP, turn face toward upper RIGHT of image. Row 2 = turn face toward LEFT edge of image, STRAIGHT forward at viewer (match reference face), turn face toward RIGHT edge of image. Row 3 = turn face toward lower LEFT of image, look straight DOWN, turn face toward lower RIGHT of image. The left-column noses point toward the LEFT edge, the right-column noses toward the RIGHT edge. Do not just shift pupils, actually turn whole head. LAYOUT: nine equal square cells in a perfectly regular 3x3 grid. Each bust occupies roughly 76 percent of cell height and 78 percent of cell width, horizontally centered, consistent head size and torso baseline in every cell. Wide empty transparent margins on ALL four sides of each cell; shoulders end well above each cell bottom; no clipping or contact with cell edges, no neighbouring busts touching. Render clean detailed high contrast grayscale halftone with crisp hair and face likeness. No text, captions, labels, borders, symbols, accessories, colored accents, watermark, or background rectangle. Output a square sprite sheet at high resolution, ideally 1536x1536.

## Expression prompt

Image 1 is the previously generated 3x3 head-angle atlas: COPY its central portrait exactly for the same person's likeness, black and white dotted photographic halftone style, suit, white shirt and diagonal striped tie. Image 2 is his original portrait for identity. Create the matching second square PNG sprite sheet: exactly 3 columns x 3 rows with NINE portraits of this same man. Unlike Image 1, EVERY portrait faces STRAIGHT FORWARD, head upright. Only FACIAL EXPRESSION changes. Keep realistic adult proportions, recognizable same face, same short wavy dark hair and faint thin moustache, no glasses, never cartoon/chibi, entirely monochrome. No text or floating symbols. Layout, head size, body size and baseline MUST match Image 1's center cell. Reuse ONE identical front-facing body/torso, precisely same shoulder shape, white shirt and tie pixels, same position and width across all nine cells. The bust should occupy 76 percent cell height, centered in its square cell, shoulder width about 78 percent cell width. ALL drawings entirely inside cell boundaries, generous genuinely transparent gutters on all four sides including below shoulders. The exact nine expressions in row-major order: 1 natural brief blink with eyes gently closed and slight smile; 2 eyes gently closed with a warmer smile; 3 pleased bright smile with slightly squinting eyes; 4 mildly surprised raised eyebrows and small open mouth; 5 subtle one-eye wink and smile; 6 bashful gentle smile eyes glancing slightly downward but head straight; 7 relaxed sleepy closed eyes; 8 mildly dazed raised eyebrows, natural eyes, no spirals; 9 broad delighted smile eyes happily narrowed. Keep expression changes tasteful and photographic, not exaggerated cartoon faces. Same monochrome halftone dot texture and precise face identity as Image 1 in every frame. No color, hearts, stars, text, borders, logos, shadows, watermarks, props, background rectangle, or partial/touching/cropped characters. Real transparent alpha PNG. Output square high-resolution atlas ideally 1536x1536.
